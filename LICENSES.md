## What is computed

The slider state, editable digit input, similarity scores, bounded fast trace, recall decision, matrix readout, and displayed values are computed live in JavaScript. Canvas layouts and staged matrix views are illustrative. The visualization is a teaching analogy, not a biological simulation or a reproduction of BDH/BDH-CQ. The unused experimental WebGL files are retained for future work and are not part of the published runtime.
