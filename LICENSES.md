## What is computed

The state update, recall decision, matrix readout, and history chart are computed live in JavaScript. WebGL node positions, motion, glow, and pointer response are illustrative. The visualization is a teaching analogy, not a biological simulation or a reproduction of BDH/BDH-CQ.
